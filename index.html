How it works

Three simple parts that work together to keep your study sessions on track.

⏱️

Pomodoro sessions

Study in 25-minute focus blocks with short breaks, and a longer break after every fourth session. All lengths are adjustable.

👁️

Smart focus detection

Your camera spots when a phone is in your hand or when you have been looking down or away for too long.

🔔

Gentle reminders

You get a sound, a voice prompt and an on-screen nudge, but never while you are on a break.

Your camera stays yours

Privacy is built in, not bolted on.

  • All detection runs on your device. Video frames are never uploaded or recorded.
  • The camera turns off the moment you press “Stop studying”.
  • Your daily stats are saved only in this browser’s local storage.
